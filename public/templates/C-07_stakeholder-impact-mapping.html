2. Stakeholder Identification — Onion Model

Identify stakeholders in concentric rings of proximity to the system. Layer 1: Direct users who interact with the system. Layer 2: People directly affected by system outputs but who don't use it themselves. Layer 3: Broader community or society affected indirectly.
