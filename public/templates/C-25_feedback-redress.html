2. Feedback Channels

Design multiple channels to accommodate different access levels. Per EU AI Act Art. 86, affected persons must be able to request explanations of decisions.

🌍 Global South consideration: Digital-only complaint systems exclude large portions of affected populations. Include non-digital channels (community health workers, village meetings, local leaders). Consider using existing community structures as feedback intermediaries.

3. Complaint Handling Process

Define a clear, documented process from complaint intake to resolution. Each step should have a responsible person and a timeline.
