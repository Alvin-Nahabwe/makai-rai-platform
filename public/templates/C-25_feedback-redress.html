C-25: Feedback & Redress Mechanisms

Procedural Control in the MAK-AI Responsible AI Toolkit

Grounding: Draws on two articles of the EU AI Act as models. Article 86 gives a right to an explanation of certain decisions made with high-risk AI systems in the EU. Article 85 gives a right to complain to a market surveillance authority, a national regulator in the EU. It also draws on the UNESCO AI Ethics Recommendation on contestability, and on research on accessible redress mechanisms in low-resource contexts.

2. Feedback Channels

Design multiple channels to accommodate different access levels. Article 86 of the EU AI Act is one model for a right to ask for an explanation of a decision. Consider whether the people your system affects should be able to ask for one, and how.

Local context: Digital-only complaint systems exclude large portions of affected populations. Include non-digital channels (community health workers, village meetings, local leaders). Consider using existing community structures as feedback intermediaries.

3. Complaint Handling Process

Define a clear, documented process from complaint intake to resolution. Each step should have a responsible person and a timeline.
