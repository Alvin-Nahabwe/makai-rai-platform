# Datasheet for Dataset

> Adapted from Gebru et al. (2018) — "Datasheets for Datasets"

---

## Motivation

- **Purpose**: What was the dataset created for?
- **Creators**: Who created the dataset and on behalf of which organization?
- **Funding**: Who funded the creation of the dataset?

## Composition

- **Instances**: What do the instances represent (e.g., patients, clinical encounters, lab results)?
- **Total count**: How many instances are in the dataset?
- **Sampling**: Does the dataset contain a sample or is it a complete collection?
- **Data types**: What data types are included (tabular, images, text, time series)?
- **Labels**: Is there a target variable? What does it represent?
- **Missing data**: Is there missing data? What is the extent and pattern?
- **Relationships**: Are there relationships between instances (e.g., multiple visits per patient)?
- **Splits**: Are there predefined train/validation/test splits?
- **Errors**: Are there known errors, noise, or redundancies?

## Collection Process

- **Mechanisms**: How was the data collected (sensors, surveys, medical records, APIs)?
- **Collectors**: Who collected the data?
- **Time frame**: Over what time period was data collected?
- **Ethical review**: Was data collection approved by an ethics committee or IRB?
- **Consent**: Was informed consent obtained from data subjects?
- **Notification**: Were data subjects notified about the data collection?

## Preprocessing / Cleaning / Labeling

- **Preprocessing**: What preprocessing/cleaning was done?
- **Raw data**: Is the raw (unprocessed) data available?
- **Software**: What software was used for preprocessing?
- **Labeling**: How were labels assigned? By whom?
- **Quality assurance**: What quality checks were applied to labels?

## Uses

- **Intended uses**: What tasks has the dataset been used for?
- **Potential uses**: What other tasks could the dataset be used for?
- **Inappropriate uses**: Are there tasks for which the dataset should NOT be used?
- **Impact considerations**: Could the dataset composition or collection process negatively impact future uses?

## Distribution

- **Distribution method**: How is the dataset distributed (API, download, on request)?
- **License**: Under what license is the dataset distributed?
- **Access controls**: Are there access restrictions?
- **Export restrictions**: Are there geographic restrictions on data sharing?

## Maintenance

- **Maintainer**: Who is maintaining the dataset?
- **Contact**: How can the maintainer be contacted?
- **Updates**: Will the dataset be updated? How often?
- **Retention**: How long will the dataset be retained?
- **Versioning**: Is there a mechanism for tracking versions?
- **Older versions**: Are older versions available?
