C-01: Alternatives Analysis

Procedural Control — MAK-AI Responsible AI Toolkit

Grounding: This template adapts the NIST AI RMF MAP 1.1 proportionality assessment and the Canada Algorithmic Impact Assessment impact-level approach. It asks practitioners to systematically evaluate whether AI/ML is the most appropriate solution before committing resources.

1. Problem Definition

Clearly articulate the problem you are trying to solve. Be specific about what outcome you need — not the technology you want to use.

2. Alternatives Comparison

List at least 3 approaches: the proposed AI/ML solution, a simpler computational method, and a non-computational approach. For each, assess feasibility, cost, and risk honestly.

3. Proportionality Assessment

Based on the comparison above, justify your choice. The goal is to ensure AI is used only when it demonstrably adds value over simpler approaches.
