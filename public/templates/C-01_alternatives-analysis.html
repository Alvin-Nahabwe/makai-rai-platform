C-01: Alternatives Analysis

Procedural Control in the MAK-AI Responsible AI Toolkit

Grounding: This template draws on NIST AI RMF MAP 1.1 and on the Canada Algorithmic Impact Assessment. MAP 1.1 is about understanding and documenting an AI system's intended purpose and context. This template asks your team to check whether AI/ML is the most appropriate solution before you commit resources.

1. Problem Definition

Clearly articulate the problem you are trying to solve. Be specific about the outcome you need, not the technology you want to use.

2. Alternatives Comparison

List at least 3 approaches: the proposed AI/ML solution, a simpler computational method, and a non-computational approach. For each, assess feasibility, cost, and risk honestly.

Local context: In resource-constrained settings, consider infrastructure requirements (reliable power, internet connectivity, GPU access), local technical capacity for maintenance, and whether simpler methods might be more sustainable long-term.

3. Is the AI approach proportionate?

Based on the comparison above, justify your choice. The goal is to ensure AI is used only when it demonstrably adds value over simpler approaches.
