## 1. About these terms

These terms are an agreement between you and Makerere University Centre for Artificial Intelligence (MAK-AI), also called "we" or "us". They cover your use of the MAK-AI RAI Platform at makrai.org (the "platform").

Read them together with the Privacy Notice. The Privacy Notice explains what personal data the platform holds, where it is stored, who receives it, and your rights.

You accept these terms when you tick the box for them before you start to use the platform. If you do not accept them, do not use the platform.

In these terms:

- "Your organisation" means the space in the platform that you were invited to. It is usually one research team.
- "Your institution" means the university, hospital or research institute that your organisation belongs to, and that is responsible for your organisation's records.
- "Records" means everything your team enters in the platform: projects, decisions and their reasons, evidence files and links, findings, mitigations, accepted risks, stage reviews, seals and the documents generated from them.
- "Owner" and "admin" mean the team roles shown with those names in the platform. The "MAK-AI administrator" is the person at MAK-AI who creates organisations and manages accounts. The MAK-AI administrator is not a member of any organisation.

## 2. Who provides the platform

The platform is provided by Makerere University Centre for Artificial Intelligence (MAK-AI), College of Computing and Information Sciences, Makerere University, P.O. Box 7062, Kampala, Uganda.

For questions about these terms or about personal data, write to privacy@makrai.org. To report a security problem, write to security@makrai.org.

## 3. What the platform does, and what it does not do

The platform helps a research team keep one record of how it handled responsible-AI questions for an AI system. For each question the team records a decision, the reason for it, who decided and when, and its evidence. The team can raise findings, record mitigations and accepted risks, seal a stage review, and generate documents from the sealed record.

The platform records what your team decides. Your team makes every decision and is responsible for it.

The platform does not:

- approve, certify, audit, rate, rank or score any AI system, project, team or person;
- check whether what your team records is true, complete or sufficient;
- give legal, ethical, medical or regulatory advice;
- run, test or evaluate any AI system, model, dataset or uploaded file.

The questions and guidance in the platform help your team think. They do not replace your team's own judgement, the advice of your institution, or the decision of an ethics committee, funder or regulator. A document generated by the platform is your team's statement, not a statement by MAK-AI.

## 4. Who may use the platform

The platform is for members of research teams at research institutions. You can join only by invitation. The MAK-AI administrator creates each organisation, records the legal name of its institution, and invites its first owner. After that, the owners and admins of an organisation invite its other members.

You may use the platform only if:

- you were invited to it at your own email address;
- you are at least 18 years old;
- you use it for your work with your organisation.

You use the platform on behalf of your organisation and your institution. Section 7 sets out what that means.

## 5. Your account

When you accept an invitation, you choose a password and give your name. Your email address is the one the invitation was sent to.

You must:

- give your real name and keep your account to yourself; an account is for one person only;
- keep your password secret and not use it for any other service;
- sign out on a computer that other people use;
- tell your organisation's owner, and us at security@makrai.org, as soon as you think someone else has used your account.

You are responsible for what is done with your account, unless it was done after you told us it had been misused.

You cannot yet change your name or email address yourself. To correct them, write to the privacy contact in the Privacy Notice. MAK-AI will make the change and tell you when it is done.

## 6. Roles in a team

Each member of an organisation has one role: owner, admin, assessor, reviewer or viewer. The role decides what the member can do. Every member, whatever the role, can read every record of the organisation, including evidence files and the names of the people who made each decision.

Owners and admins manage the team. They invite and remove members, change roles, and can download a copy of all the organisation's records. Only an owner can make another member an owner.

The last owner with an active account cannot leave the organisation, or delete their account, until another member has been made an owner.

The first owner of an organisation accepts these terms for the institution as well as for themselves. Each owner who follows does the same. If your institution has signed a written agreement with MAK-AI about the platform, that agreement wins where it differs from these terms.

## 7.2 Your institution's responsibility for the records

Your institution decides, through your organisation's owners, what its team records, why, and who may read it. Your institution is responsible for:

- what the records say, and for having the right to put each record, file and link into the platform;
- choosing who becomes a member, and what role each member has;
- removing members who should no longer read the records;
- any approval, permission or notice that the law, a funder or an ethics committee requires for its research or for its records.

## 7.3 Who controls the data in the records

Your institution is the controller of the personal data in your organisation's records. That means your institution decides why and how that data is used. MAK-AI acts as its service provider. In data protection law this is called a "processor" or "operator": a company that handles data only on someone else's instructions. MAK-AI handles that data only to provide the platform to your institution.

MAK-AI is the controller of account data and of the data it needs to run and protect the platform: names, email addresses, passwords, records of which legal texts each person accepted, and security logs. The Privacy Notice explains this in full.

## 8. How you may use the platform

Use the platform only for lawful work on responsible AI for your organisation.

Do not:

- put into the platform any dataset, or any data about patients, research participants or other people your AI system is about. Describe your data. Do not upload it.
- put into the platform more personal data about anyone than the record needs. Where a person outside the team matters to a decision, name their role, not the person, unless there is a reason to name them.
- upload a file that you do not have the right to share with your team, or a file that contains harmful software;
- try to read records of an organisation you are not a member of, or try to get around the limits of your role;
- try to break, overload or test the security of the platform, unless MAK-AI has agreed to it in writing;
- share your password or let another person use your account;
- use the platform to harass anyone, or to store anything unlawful.

If you find a security weakness, report it to security@makrai.org and do not use it.

## 9. What you put in, and who owns it

Your organisation's records belong to your institution, or to the people who own them under your institution's own rules. MAK-AI claims no ownership of them.

Your institution, and each member who adds content, allows MAK-AI to store, copy, show and send that content only as needed to provide the platform: for example, to show records to members, generate documents, make backups and produce the exports described in section 13.

Decisions, evidence, findings, mitigations, accepted risks, stage reviews, seals and generated documents are kept with their full history. A change to one of them is saved as a new version, and the earlier version stays. No member can delete an evidence file. This is how the platform lets a team show later what it decided and when. The one exception is erasure on an owner's written instruction, which the Privacy Notice describes.

A project's name, description and details are different. A member whose role allows it can change them, and the earlier wording is not kept. The same is true of the wording a team prepares for a document before the document is generated. An owner or admin can delete a project that does not yet hold any record.

Evidence files may be PDF, PNG or JPEG files, or ZIP files (which include Word, Excel and PowerPoint files), of up to 10 MB each. The platform stores each file as it is. It checks only the first few bytes of a file to confirm its type. It does not open, preview or run a file. When a member downloads a file, it is saved to their computer and is not opened by the platform. Open a file only if you trust the member who added it. A link is stored as text. The platform does not visit it.

## 10. What MAK-AI can see

The MAK-AI administrator creates organisations and manages accounts. The MAK-AI administrator has no access to any organisation's records in the platform: it cannot open a project, decision, evidence file, finding, review or document, or count them. It can see:

- the list of organisations, with the date each was created and how many members it has;
- the invitations sent to each organisation's first owner, with the email address, whether the invitation was used, and its dates;
- each organisation's name and the legal name of its institution, recorded when the organisation was created, and, for organisations created before this version of the terms, the data protection contact email given then;
- the list of accounts, with each person's name, email address, platform role, the date the account was created, whether it is active, and whether the person is the only active owner of an organisation.

The people at MAK-AI who run the server can technically reach everything stored on it. MAK-AI's rule is that they read records only where it is needed to keep the platform working, to restore a backup, to carry out an owner's written instruction, or because the law requires it. Their ordinary access to the server needs a key and a second step, and is logged on the server and off it, but it is not reported to organisations. If one of them signs in at the server's emergency console, the owners and admins of every organisation are told by email.

## 12. Suspension and ending an account

You can delete your account at any time on the My account page. The Privacy Notice explains what deletion removes and what stays. (The MAK-AI administrator's account cannot be deleted this way. It is handed over to another person at MAK-AI.)

An owner or admin of your organisation can remove you from the organisation. You then lose access to its records. You stay a member of any other organisation, and your account stays until you delete it.

Only MAK-AI can deactivate an account. A deactivated account cannot sign in to any organisation and gets no reminder emails. Its records stay with each organisation. Because deactivation affects every organisation the person belongs to, MAK-AI deactivates an account only:

- on the written request of an owner, if the person belongs to no other organisation. If they do, the owner can remove them from their own organisation instead;
- if the account is used in breach of section 8, or to protect the platform or other users from harm;
- if the law requires it.

Where it is safe and lawful to do so, MAK-AI will tell the person, and the owners of their organisations, why the account was deactivated, and will reactivate it if the reason no longer applies.

## 13. Leaving the platform

## 13.1 When you leave

Before you delete your account, you can download a copy of your personal data on the My account page. The records you added stay with your organisation, because they belong to your institution. After you delete your account, they are shown as made by "Deleted user".

## 13.2 When your organisation leaves

Your organisation can stop using the platform at any time. Before it does, an owner or admin can download the team export on the organisation's page. The export is one file that holds:

- every project, with every version of every decision and its reason, every finding and its history, every mitigation, every accepted risk, every stage review and seal, and the record of every generated document;
- every evidence file, and every generated document as a PDF;
- the list of members, with their names and roles;
- a short guide to what the file contains.

The main contents are in a format a computer can read, so your institution can keep them or move them to another system.

## 13.3 Deleting your organisation's records

The platform has no button that deletes an organisation. To have its records deleted, an owner writes to privacy@makrai.org and asks for it. MAK-AI will check that the request comes from an owner and agree a date with the owners in writing, leaving them time to download the team export. MAK-AI will delete the organisation's records by that date, and confirm in writing when it is done.

Some data is kept afterwards:

- The accounts of the organisation's members. An account belongs to the person, who may also be a member of another organisation. Each person can delete their own account (section 12).
- Each member's record of the legal texts they accepted, to show what was agreed.
- A record that the organisation existed: its name, the legal name of its institution, and when the organisation was created and its records deleted. MAK-AI keeps this to show what was agreed and that the records were deleted on request.
- Security logs, for up to 90 days, and backups, until they are deleted on their own schedule, as the Privacy Notice describes.

If MAK-AI stops providing the platform, it will tell the owners of each organisation in writing at least 90 days before, so that they can download their records. If something outside MAK-AI's control makes that impossible, it will tell them as early as it can.
