## What we collect

Account details (name, email), the projects and AI-system metadata you enter, and your assessment responses and generated reports.

## How we use it

To provide the assessment service to you: saving and resuming assessments, generating reports, and showing your history. Everything entered in an organization can be read by every member of that organization.

## Your controls

There is no self-service export or account deletion in the interface yet. Consent records are stored with a timestamp, so that what was accepted, and when, can be shown later.

## Security

Passwords are stored hashed. Data entered in an organization can be read by every member of that organization and by the platform operators. See the Terms of Service for acceptable use.

## Where personal data goes outside the host

Cloudflare: Web traffic to and from the platform. Cloudflare terminates TLS and so sees traffic in clear at its edge. Where: Cloudflare's edge network.

Resend: The email messages the platform sends, with the recipient's address. Where: United States.

Off-site backup store: Copies of the database backups. Where: The backup provider's storage, named by the owner.
