## 1. About this notice

This notice explains what personal data the MAK-AI RAI Platform (the "platform") holds about you, why, where it is stored, who receives it, how long it is kept, and what rights you have.

It is written for everyone who uses the platform: the members of research teams, and the MAK-AI administrator. Read it together with the Terms of Service. Words used in both, such as "organisation", "institution", "owner" and "admin", have the same meaning in both.

## 2. Who is responsible for your data

The platform is run by Makerere University Centre for Artificial Intelligence (MAK-AI), College of Computing and Information Sciences, Makerere University, P.O. Box 7062, Kampala, Uganda. In this notice it is also called "we" or "us".

Who is responsible depends on the data:

- Your account, and the running of the platform: MAK-AI is responsible (the "controller") for your account data, the record of which legal texts you accepted, and the data it needs to run and protect the platform, such as security logs.
- Your team's records: your institution is responsible (the controller) for the personal data in your organisation's records: projects, decisions and reasons, evidence files and links, findings, mitigations, accepted risks, stage reviews and generated documents. MAK-AI handles that data only on your institution's behalf, to provide the platform to it. In data protection law this makes MAK-AI its "processor" or "operator": a company that handles data only on someone else's instructions.

To contact MAK-AI about personal data, write to privacy@makrai.org. MAK-AI has not appointed a data protection officer, or a representative in any country.

To contact your institution about your organisation's records, write to your organisation's owners. MAK-AI also writes to them when it needs to reach your institution.

## 3. Where your data is stored and who receives it

Please read this section before you accept this notice and before you tick the box for storage abroad.

## 3.1 Where your data is stored

The platform's server, and everything it holds, is in Finland, in the European Union. This includes your account, your team's records, every evidence file, and the copies of the database backups. Some of your data also passes through, or is handled by, companies in the United States and in other countries, as 3.2 explains.

So if you live in any country other than Finland, your personal data is stored and handled outside your country. MAK-AI keeps no copy of it in your country.

## 3.2 The companies that store or carry your data

MAK-AI uses these companies to run the platform. Each receives only what is listed, and only to provide its service to MAK-AI.

Hetzner: The server that runs the platform and holds its database: all accounts, records and evidence files. Where: Helsinki, Finland.

Hetzner Storage Box: Copies of the database backups. Where: Finland.

Cloudflare: All web traffic between your browser and the platform, including your IP address and the pages, records and files you send and receive. Cloudflare can read this traffic as it passes. Where: Cloudflare's data centres around the world, usually the one nearest to you.

Resend: The emails the platform sends, with the recipient's email address. Where: United States.

No other company receives personal data from the platform. The platform has no advertising, no analytics and no tracking service, and it does not send your data to any AI service.

## 3.3 The grounds MAK-AI relies on

MAK-AI stores and sends your data abroad on the grounds below.

1. Your consent. Before you start to use the platform, you are asked to tick a separate box, the consent to storage abroad. When you tick it, you agree that your personal data is stored and handled in the countries named in 3.1 and 3.2, by the companies named in 3.2.

The risks of agreeing: the laws of Finland, the United States and the other countries in 3.2 may not be the law of your country, and may protect your data less, or differently. Courts and authorities in those countries may be able to require access to data held there. If something goes wrong, it may be harder for you to use your rights across borders.

2. It is needed to provide the platform. The platform runs on one server in Finland. It cannot be provided to you or your institution without storing your data there and using the companies in 3.2.
3. The protection in the receiving country. Finland applies the European Union's General Data Protection Regulation, and Hetzner is bound by it.

The laws of some countries require an approval from the data protection authority, or a notice to it, before data leaves the country, or require a copy of the data to be kept in the country. MAK-AI has not obtained such an approval in any country. MAK-AI itself keeps the data only in Finland. Your institution should check what its own law requires, before its organisation is created and while it uses the platform.

## 3.4 If you do not agree

You do not have to tick the box for storage abroad, but you cannot use the platform without it: there is no way to use the platform with your data kept only in your own country.

You can withdraw your consent at any time by deleting your account (section 9.4). If you withdraw, what was done with your data before stays lawful. After you withdraw, the records you added stay with your organisation, because your institution is responsible for them, as section 9.4 explains.

## 3.5 Your institution's own duties

As controller of its records, your institution is responsible for any notice to, or approval from, its data protection authority that its law requires for those records, and for any impact assessment. MAK-AI will give it the information it needs.

## 3.6 If this changes

MAK-AI will not itself store your data in another country, or use another company to store or carry it, without first publishing a new version of this notice. You will be asked to accept the new version, and to tick the box for storage abroad again, before you continue to use the platform. Cloudflare and Resend may use companies of their own, under their own terms.

## 4. What data the platform holds

MAK-AI collects this data because the platform needs it. No law requires MAK-AI to collect it.

## 4.1 Your account

- Your name, which you give, and your email address, which the person who invited you gave.
- Your password, stored only as a scrambled form (a "hash") that cannot be turned back into the password.
- Your role on the platform, and the organisations you belong to, with your role in each and the date you joined.
- Whether your account is active, whether you must change your password, whether you want reminder emails, and which organisation you opened last.
- Counts of failed sign-in attempts and the time an account or browser is locked after too many of them.
- A counter that changes when all your sign-ins are ended, for example after a password change.
- The dates your account was created and last changed.
- An old research-consent setting, which the platform no longer uses. Where it was ever set, it is marked void.

## 4.2 What you accepted

For each legal text you accept: which text, its version, a fingerprint of its exact words, and the date and time. A fingerprint is a code worked out from the exact words, which changes if any word changes. No IP address is stored with it.

## 4.3 Sign-in and security data

- One record for each browser you sign in from, with the time it was last used and its count of failed sign-ins. It does not hold your IP address or the name of your browser.
- A record of each password reset you asked for, with its times. The reset link itself is not stored, only a fingerprint of it.
- Security logs: a line for each sign-in, sign-out, password change and important action, with your account number and the time. They do not hold your email address or the text of any record.
- The web server's log: for each request to the platform, your IP address, the time, the page asked for and the result. It does not hold your browser's name or the page you came from.

## 4.4 Your team's records

Your organisation's records show the name of each member who entered or made a decision, raised a finding, owns a mitigation, accepted a risk, sealed a review or generated a document, and when they did it. Members can see this.

The records also hold whatever your team types or uploads: reasons, notes, finding descriptions, evidence files and links. These may mention people, including people who do not use the platform. Your institution decides what goes in and is responsible for it.

The documents the platform generates print the names of the people who made decisions, own mitigations, accepted risks and sealed the review.

Do not put datasets, or data about patients, research participants or other people your AI system is about, into the platform. The Terms of Service forbid it.

## 4.5 Invitations

An invitation holds the invited person's email address, the organisation, the role offered, who sent it, when it was sent and when it expires, whether it was used and when, and whether the sender chose to see the invitation link on screen. An invitation made at an institution's written request also holds a reference to that request. Owners and admins of the organisation can see the invitations it sent.

## 4.6 Organisation details

For each organisation: its name, its members and their roles, and the legal name of the institution responsible for it, which MAK-AI records when it creates the organisation. MAK-AI does not record a separate data protection contact for the institution. It writes to the organisation's owners instead. For organisations created before this version of the notice, MAK-AI keeps the data protection contact email that was given then; it does not ask for one any more.

## 4.7 Emails

The platform sends these emails:

- an invitation to join an organisation;
- a notice that your account was created;
- a password reset link, only when you ask for one;
- a notice each time your password is changed;
- reminders when a mitigation you own is due, and when it is 7 days late. You can turn these off on My account;
- a notice to the owners and admins of every organisation if a person who runs the server signs in at its emergency console.

No email holds the text of any record.

## 5. Why the data is used, and the lawful basis

Creating and running your account, signing you in, and sending the emails in 4.7.
Lawful basis: needed to provide the platform you and your institution asked for. Where your country's law requires consent, your consent when you tick the box for this notice at sign-up, and for storage abroad, your consent when you tick the separate box.

Keeping the platform and its users safe: stopping repeated wrong passwords, finding misuse, fixing faults.
Lawful basis: MAK-AI's legitimate interest (a reason the law accepts) in keeping the service secure, and its legal duty to keep personal data secure.

Showing what you accepted, and when.
Lawful basis: MAK-AI's legal duty to be able to show that it informed you and had your consent, and its legitimate interest in showing what was agreed.

Storing and showing your team's records, generating documents, making exports.
Lawful basis: your institution's lawful basis, as controller. MAK-AI acts on its instructions.

Backups, so data can be recovered after a fault.
Lawful basis: needed to provide the platform, and MAK-AI's legal duty to keep personal data available and secure.

MAK-AI does not use your data for any other purpose. It does not sell it, does not use it for advertising, does not use it for research, does not use it to train any AI system, and does not use it to make automated decisions about you.

You must have a name, an email address and a password to have an account. Without them the platform cannot be provided to you.

## 6. Who can see your data

- Members of your organisation can read every record of the organisation, including the names of the people in it. They can see the list of members, with names, roles and join dates, but not members' email addresses.
- Owners and admins of your organisation can also see the invitations it sent, with email addresses, and a list of changes to its members and settings.
- The MAK-AI administrator creates organisations and manages accounts. It sees the list of organisations, with each one's creation date and number of members; the invitations sent to each organisation's first owner, with email addresses and dates; each organisation's name and the legal name of its institution, and, for organisations created before this version of the notice, the data protection contact email given then; and the list of accounts, with each person's name, email address, platform role, creation date, whether the account is active, and whether the person is the only active owner of an organisation. It has no access to any organisation's records in the platform.
- The people at MAK-AI who run the server can technically reach everything stored on it, including records. MAK-AI's rule is that they look at personal data only to keep the platform working, to restore a backup, to carry out an owner's written instruction, or because the law requires it. Their ordinary access needs a key and a second step, and is logged on the server and off it, but it is not reported to organisations. If one of them signs in at the server's emergency console, the owners and admins of every organisation are told by email.
- The companies in section 3.2, as described there.
- A court or public authority, only where the law requires MAK-AI to give it data. Where the data is in an organisation's records, MAK-AI will tell the organisation's owners first, unless the law forbids it.

The documents your team generates are meant to be given to others, such as an ethics committee or funder. Once your team gives a document to someone, that person's own rules apply to their copy.

## 7. How long data is kept

Your account: while your account exists. When you delete it, your name and email address are removed from it at once (section 9.4).

What you accepted (4.2): kept for as long as the platform runs, including after your account is deleted, so that MAK-AI can show what was agreed. No job deletes it. Once your account is deleted it is linked only to an account number.

Password reset records (4.3): kept for as long as the platform runs, including after your account is deleted, linked only to the account number. No job deletes them. The reset link stops working after 30 minutes, or once used.

Browser records (4.3): kept for as long as the platform runs, including after your account is deleted. No job deletes them. A record not used for 30 days may be taken over the next time you sign in from a browser the platform does not know.

Security logs and web server logs (4.3): 90 days, then deleted automatically.

Your team's records (4.4), invitations (4.5) and organisation details (4.6): while your organisation uses the platform. When it leaves, as the Terms of Service, section 13.3, describe.

Backups: each backup is deleted automatically, from the server after about 31 days and from the off-site store, including its own daily snapshots, after about 43 days. Until then it still holds the data it was made from, including data deleted since. Backups are used only to recover from a fault.

A team export file on the server: deleted once it has been downloaded, and normally after 6 hours if it is not.

Unsaved text in your browser (section 8): see section 8.

## 8. Cookies and storage in your browser

The platform itself sets only the cookies it needs to work. It sets no cookie for advertising, analytics or tracking, and loads nothing from any other website.

- Sign-in cookie: keeps you signed in. You are signed out after 12 hours unless you choose "Stay signed in" when asked, and in any case after 7 days.
- Security cookies: one protects the sign-in form from forged requests. One remembers where to take you after you sign in.
- Known-browser cookie: lets the platform count failed sign-ins for each browser, so that someone guessing your password from another computer does not lock you out. It lasts 30 days.

The platform also keeps some things in your browser's own storage:

- Unsaved text: if you type a decision or a mitigation and it is not yet saved, for example because your connection drops, the text is kept in your browser so you do not lose it. It is deleted when it is saved, when you press Sign out, when another person signs in on the same browser, or when you delete your account. After 7 days the platform stops offering it, and deletes it the next time a question list or decision form is opened in that browser. If you are signed out automatically, or you change your password, the text stays until one of those things happens. It is not locked against other people who use the same computer and browser account, so press Sign out on a shared computer.
- Your choice of light or dark display: this holds no personal data.
- Short-lived notes that last only until you close the tab: for example, that you chose to read a new legal text later.

## 9. Your rights, and how to use them

Your rights depend on the law of your country. In general you have the right to know what data is held about you, to get a copy, to have wrong data corrected, to have data deleted, to object to or limit its use, to take your data elsewhere, to withdraw consent, and to complain. This section says how to use each one in the platform.

## 9.1 To be told

This notice is how MAK-AI tells you. Every version is kept on the platform's legal texts page.

## 9.2 To get a copy of your data

On the My account page, choose to download your data. You get a file, in a common format a computer can read, that holds:

- your account details (but not your password);
- your memberships;
- every legal text you accepted, and when;
- your browser records and password reset records;
- a list of the records in your current organisations that record you as their author, decider, owner or accepter, and of invitations sent to your address, with their type and date. It lists the records. It does not copy their text, and it does not find text that mentions your name.

To read the records themselves, open them in the platform. An owner or admin of your organisation can also download a complete copy of its records.

To ask for anything the download does not hold, write to privacy@makrai.org.

## 9.3 To correct your data

You cannot yet change your name or email address yourself. To correct either, write to privacy@makrai.org from the email address on your account.

To correct something in your team's records, change it in the platform if your role allows it. A change to a decision, finding, mitigation, accepted risk or review is saved as a new version, and the earlier version stays. To have earlier text removed, ask your organisation (9.5). A change to a project's name, description or details replaces the earlier wording, which is not kept.

## 9.4 To delete your account

On the My account page, choose to delete your account. You confirm by typing a phrase and your password. Deletion happens at once and cannot be undone. Download your data first if you want a copy.

When you delete your account:

- your name is replaced by "Deleted user" and your email address is removed from your account;
- your password stops working and you are signed out everywhere;
- you leave every organisation you belonged to;
- unsaved text is removed from the browser you use to delete it.

Some data stays after your account is deleted. Each item, and why:

- The records you entered stay with your organisation and are shown as made by "Deleted user". They are your organisation's record of what its team decided, and your institution is responsible for them.
- Text that your team typed and that names you stays, because it is part of those records. Your organisation can have it erased (9.5).
- Documents generated before you deleted your account still print your name. A generated document is stored once and not changed, so that it still matches the copies the team gave to others.
- Your email address stays in the record of each invitation sent to you, which the organisation's owners and admins can see. It is part of the organisation's record of who was invited. The platform cannot yet remove it; write to privacy@makrai.org if you want it removed. Invitations you sent are shown as sent by "Deleted user".
- The record of each legal text you accepted, and when, stays, linked only to an account number. It shows what was agreed, which MAK-AI and your institution may need to prove later.
- Security logs and backups that hold your data are deleted on their own schedule (section 7).

If you are the last owner of an organisation with an active account, you must first make another member an owner. The MAK-AI administrator's account cannot be deleted this way; it is handed over to another person at MAK-AI.

## 9.5 To have data in a team's records erased or corrected

Your institution is responsible for the records, so ask your organisation's owner. If your institution agrees, an owner sends MAK-AI a written instruction, and MAK-AI erases the text or file and confirms in writing when it is done. The record keeps a note that something was erased, and when. Any generated document that printed the erased content is also erased.

Not every field can be erased this way. A project's name and description can be changed in the platform by a member whose role allows it, and the old wording is then gone. The names of organisations and of people cannot be erased this way. For those, write to privacy@makrai.org and MAK-AI will tell you and your organisation what can be done. If you write to MAK-AI about a team's records, MAK-AI will pass your request to the organisation's owners.

## 9.6 To object, or to limit use

To object to a use of your data, or to ask that its use be limited while a question is settled, write to privacy@makrai.org. For data in a team's records, write to your organisation's owner.

## 9.7 To withdraw consent

You can withdraw your consent to storage abroad by deleting your account (9.4). For the reasons in section 3.4, you cannot keep using the platform after withdrawing it.

## 9.8 To stop reminder emails

Turn them off on the My account page.

## 9.9 How MAK-AI answers

MAK-AI answers requests free of charge, as soon as it can and within the time set by the law of your country. It may ask you to show that the request comes from you, for example by writing from the email address on your account.

## 10. Security

MAK-AI protects your data in these ways:

- All traffic to the platform is encrypted between your browser and Cloudflare, and again between Cloudflare and the server.
- Passwords are stored only as a hash. After 5 wrong passwords, sign-in to the account is paused for 15 minutes. A browser you have signed in from in the last 30 days keeps its own count, so someone guessing on another computer does not lock you out.
- Each organisation's records are kept apart in the database, so that only its own members can read them through the platform.
- Backups are encrypted before they are stored. The copies kept off the server are stored without the key.
- Remote access to the server needs a key and a second step, and is logged. Every sign-in at the server's emergency console, which is reached through the hosting company's own website, is reported to every organisation.
- Evidence files are stored, not opened or run.

The database on the server is not itself encrypted. No system is perfectly safe. If MAK-AI learns that someone got access to personal data who should not have, it will tell the organisations affected and, where the law requires it, the data protection authority and the people affected.

## 11. Complaints

If you are unhappy with how your data is handled, write first to privacy@makrai.org, or to your organisation's owner for its records. MAK-AI will try to put it right.

You can also complain at any time to the data protection authority of your own country. If you do not know which authority that is, write to privacy@makrai.org and MAK-AI will help you find it.
