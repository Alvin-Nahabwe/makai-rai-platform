### 1b. Criterion 1, methods in plain words — **change**

Cell 10 ("### Step 3: during training, `ExponentiatedGradient`"). "each time giving more weight to the people the last model treated worst on the chosen rule" misdescribes the algorithm: the weights (and, for some examples, the training label) change by group from Lagrange multipliers on the current mix of models, pushing an over-selected group down as well as an under-selected one up. "`TOLERANCE` says how far from perfectly equal it may settle" reads as a guarantee on the reported gap; `eps` bounds each group's rate against the overall rate on the training data, and the executed test gap is 0.022 with tolerance 0.01.

Replace the "**What this does.**" paragraph:

> **What this does.** It trains the model **with the fairness rule inside the training**. Fairlearn fits a series of logistic regressions, each time giving more weight to the people the last model treated worst on the chosen rule (equal selection rates, or equal error rates), and combines them into one randomised model. `TOLERANCE` says how far from perfectly equal it may settle. Predictions use a seed, because the combined model flips a weighted coin between its parts.

with:

> **What this does.** It trains the model **with the fairness rule inside the training**. Fairlearn fits a series of logistic regressions. Before each fit it changes how much each training example counts, group by group (and for some examples the outcome the model is trained toward), so that the next model is pushed toward the chosen rule (equal selection rates, or equal error rates) wherever the models so far break it; it then combines them into one randomised model. `TOLERANCE` is how far each group's rate may sit from the rate for everyone, on the training data; it is a target, not a guarantee, and the gap on the test set can be larger. Predictions use a seed, because the combined model flips a weighted coin between its parts.

The other method explanations stand: cell 8 (`CorrelationRemover`: linear part removed, group columns dropped, strength 0 to 1, matches the docstring), cell 12 (`ThresholdOptimizer`: per-group thresholds, randomisation between two, group needed at use), cell 14 (`plot_model_comparison`), cell 16 (adversarial family needs PyTorch or TensorFlow).
