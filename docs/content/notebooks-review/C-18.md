### 3b. change. Cell 20, "### Step 8: noise and small changes (the robustness check)", "What it does not show"
**Criterion 3** (each step's "does not show" must be honest).
**Finding.** The small-change table in the executed copy shows exactly 0.0000 for `instalment_rate`, `residence_since`, `existing_credits` and `people_liable`.
