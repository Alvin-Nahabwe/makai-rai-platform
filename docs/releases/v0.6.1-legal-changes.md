# What changed in the legal texts, version 1 to version 2

Sent by MAK-AI to everyone who already had an account when the platform began to ask for version 2 of these texts.
The platform asks you to read and accept the three texts before you continue.

## Terms of Service

The terms now say who provides the platform, how to contact us, and that the law of Uganda applies. They say
that the platform records your team's decisions and does not approve or vouch for any AI system. They explain
what it means to use the platform for your institution, what each team role can do, and what MAK-AI promises
when it handles your team's records. They also say how an account or an organisation's records can be deleted,
and how the terms will change in future.

## Privacy Notice

The earlier notice said you could not download your data or delete your account. You now can, on the My
account page, and the notice explains what stays after deletion and why. It now says who is responsible for
your data. It names the companies that store or carry your data, including the one that runs the server,
and where each one is. It also says how long each kind of data is kept, and how to use each of your
rights. Finally, it describes the cookies and browser storage the platform uses, and how to complain.

## Consent to storage abroad

The earlier version of this text held no real content. It now says plainly that your data is stored in
Finland, and that Cloudflare, around the world, and Resend, in the United States, carry or handle it. It states
the main risk of this, and that you can withdraw your consent by deleting your account.
