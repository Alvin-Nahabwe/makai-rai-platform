## What is released

v0.7.0 is the redesign of the platform's screens, built on v0.6.2 (tag `v0.6.2`), which is the release the host runs now. The version in `package.json` is `0.7.0`. The checked release commit is the merge of the redesign's integration branch at 128e3d7d into `release/0.7.0`; the commits after it change only these notes, their test and the claims markers file.

Every screen is reworded and restyled. Home, the project page, the project form, All questions and the question page (for those who record and for those who only read) are also restructured, and the Guidance and About pages are reworded. A set of faults found in a walk of the workshop journey on 14 November 2026 is fixed. Nothing stored changes: no stored decision, finding, seal or issued document is changed by this release, and no legal text changes, so nobody is asked to accept the texts again.

### What a person will see

- **A new frame and a new look.** A new typeface (Overpass) and a shared set of buttons, fields, messages and page headers. A side menu on each signed-in page leads to Home, Projects, Findings, Guidance and Help; Findings opens on the project the person last opened. Every page has its own title in the browser tab.
- **Help.** A new Help page explains the words the platform uses and what sealing means, including that the platform keeps a seal and that no outside body time-stamps it. It opens for everyone, signed in or not, and for a person who has not yet accepted the legal texts.
- **Signing in and joining.** The sign-in, password, invitation and acceptance screens are reworded and restyled, and so is the way the legal texts are shown (the texts themselves are unchanged).
- **My account**, the organisation chooser, the error pages, the fixed messages of the platform's requests and the platform's emails are reworded. So is the email sent when emergency access to the server is used.
- **Findings and mitigations.** The findings list and a finding's page are reworded and restyled. The lists now come before the form that raises a finding.
- **Review and seal.** The screens to open a stage review, the review and the seal page are reworded and restyled.
- **Documents.** The documents screen is reworded and restyled ("Create a document"), and the words printed in a new data card, model card and mitigation plan are reworded, including the statement of what a document is and is not. Documents already issued keep their bytes. A document no longer fails to build when one of its findings was closed because its decision changed or was withdrawn (register D-408).
- **Download records.** The pages that download an organisation's or a project's records are reworded and restyled. The downloaded file is unchanged.
- **Members, Organisation details and the administrator's pages** are reworded and restyled.
- **Sections the copy review ruled out are removed.** About loses "Why there is no score", "What this platform never does" and the UNCST table, and its "Free toolkits" text becomes the introduction of the controls page (`/explore/controls`); the UNCST table also leaves the documents screen. Home and the project page lose their East Africa Time note, and the "Show UTC" control and the content version line leave the screen that showed them. Times on some other screens are still labelled "EAT".

### The restructured screens

- **Home: "Your next step".** For an Owner, an Admin or an Assessor, Home names one question to record next, says why it was chosen ("You last saved in {stage} on {day}. This is the next question there with no decision." and the like), and offers "Continue recording" and "Choose another question". An Assessor who has never saved is given the first question with no decision. Where the card shows, "New project" becomes the quieter button. A Reviewer and a Viewer do not get the card.
- **The project page as three stage blocks.** Each stage is a plain block, no longer a panel that opens and closes: its name and question count; "Still open in this stage" (only the counts above zero, the open findings count a link to that stage's findings, or "Nothing is open in this stage."), with the mitigations to follow up; "Recorded so far", one line per kind of decision; "Stage reviews"; and "All questions in this stage". From a 1280 px window the three blocks sit side by side; narrower, they stack in stage order.
- **"Start recording" and "Continue recording" on the project page.** An Owner, an Admin or an Assessor of a project that is not archived has one main button, first in the row under the heading: "Start recording" before they have saved a decision in the project, then "Continue recording", which opens the next question with no decision after their last save there. It opens the same question as Home's card when their newest save is in this project. A Reviewer, a Viewer and an archived project have no such button.
- **The project form in two sections.** "About the project" (name, AI system type, description) and "More details (optional)", behind "Show more details" on a new project and open on edit. Choosing "Other" as the AI system type shows a box, "Describe the type of AI system", whose text is saved. Start and end dates are three boxes (Day, Month, Year). An empty name or a bad date is listed in a summary at the top and shown beside its field; the browser's own bubble no longer appears. Team size is a text box whose wrong value is answered by the server's message beside it.
- **All questions with a status mark per question.** Under each stage's count, the stage as a row of marks, one per question, each a link, with the tally in words. Each question is a card with its words as the link, its code, its status mark in words and one line of its state. The "Show" filter is a row of chips. A browser that cannot keep a copy of unsaved text now says so under the filter.
- **The question page's frame.** The question in words is the page's heading, with its code under it. The strip reads "Previous question", "Question {n} of {m} in {stage}", "Next question" and "All questions" ("All questions" replaces "Back to {stage}"). "Sealed reviews" shows only for a question that is in a seal.
- **The route column beside the question, from a 1160 px window (the owner's decision, "Route column").** From 1160 px the stage's questions are a column to the left of the question and its form: the row of marks with the open question ringed, the "Show" filter, one section per topic with the open one marked "You are here", and "All questions in this stage". From 1160 to under 1440 px the guidance ("What good looks like", "Why this is asked") sits below the form; from 1440 px it is a third column. Under 1160 px only the row of marks, its tally and the link show, after the guidance. Choosing a filter adds `?show=` to the address. After a save the marks update.
- **The question page for a Reviewer and a Viewer reads, it does not offer a form.** A Reviewer, a Viewer, and every role in an archived project, see "What the team decided" (the mark, the version, the reason, who decided and who entered it, the evidence, the number of versions) and, when anything is open, "Still open on this question" with each finding and its open mitigations. A Reviewer keeps "Raise a finding about this question". Those who may record see the form as before.
- **Guidance and About reworded.** The framework page is "The question framework", counts in "topics" (not "modules") and gives the owner's sentence on principles word for word. The controls page opens with the vocabulary's definition of a control. About is "About the MAK-AI Responsible AI (RAI) Platform", says plainly what the platform does and what each stage means, and carries the owner's sentence on who can read an organisation's records word for word. Its band no longer moves and its cards lose their colour stripe.

### Fixes from the walk of the workshop journey

Each says whether v0.6.2, the version the host runs now, has the same fault, as the fix's close report found it.

- **An issued document's title is no longer overprinted** by the line under it. In v0.6.2: yes, every issued PDF. Documents already issued keep their stored bytes, so they keep the overprinted title; only documents issued from now on are right.
- **No raw keys in documents.** The AI system type prints the project form's own word ("Classification", not "classification"), and a seal line prints the role's word ("(Owner)", not "(owner)"). In v0.6.2: yes.
- **A half-filled document field names what is missing (the owner's decision, "Name the missing part").** An empty box beside a value prints nothing; a question with no decision inside a field that has another value prints "No decision is recorded for question {code}."; a field with nothing in it prints "Not recorded" once. In v0.6.2: yes ("Not recorded" printed beside a value).
- **A decision's closing record is printed under its finding**, as "Closed by {name} on {day}." and its cause, and only when that record closed the finding; it is no longer printed or counted as a mitigation. Not in v0.6.2: there such a document could not be built at all (D-408); the misprint came with this release's own D-408 fix and is replaced here.
- **"Close finding" is no longer offered while a mitigation is open.** The finding's page says the finding stays open while a mitigation is Planned or In progress, and what to do. A finding already stuck this way (the close was pressed earlier) shows the same sentence and a way on. In v0.6.2: yes; the deployed database may hold findings stuck this way. The server still accepts such a close; only the page changed.
- **A closed finding shows a closure line, not a mitigation.** A finding closed by its decision reads "Closed" with "Closed by {name} on {date}." and the cause, and its history says "Finding closed by {name}."; "Closed by a mitigation" stays for a finding a real mitigation closed. The findings list has a third heading, "Ready to close: the question no longer applies". The question page says when its finding can now be closed, and Home's open findings count is a link. In v0.6.2: yes (the record was drawn as an empty mitigation card).
- **Save decision waits for an evidence link that is being added**, and is sent with it; if the link is not added, nothing is sent and the form says so. In v0.6.2: yes. Only this first part of the fix is in the release: see "Known for the next release".
- **The side menu keeps its organisation on Help, My account and Guidance.** Projects, Findings, Members and Organisation details there now lead to the person's organisation, not to Home. In v0.6.2: yes. **A person held at a gate** (a password change due, or the legal texts not yet accepted) sees no organisation in the side menu, only Guidance, Help and My account. Not in v0.6.2: the organisation addresses there came with this release's fix above.
- **The invitation result comes into view** after Invite or Resend on Members, and takes focus. In v0.6.2: yes.
- **The stage review leads on.** The review, open and seal pages have a breadcrumb; empty headings are gone; an unsealed review says "Not sealed yet. An Owner, an Admin or a Reviewer seals this review."; each issued document of the review that is not erased links to its PDF, for every role, as the documents screen does. In v0.6.2: yes.
- **One sign-in refusal sentence.** A wrong email or password is answered "The email or password is wrong. Check them and try again. After five wrong tries an account is locked for 15 minutes. You can also reset your password." (it was "Try again in 15 minutes" after one wrong try). In v0.6.2: yes. During a real lock the right password gets the same sentence, by the rule of one text for every refusal.
- **Plain labels.** Separators no longer begin a line; the control cards read "Type: Procedural", not "TYPE: PROCEDURAL"; "account created from a copied link" is on its own line under the member's name; each status choice has one symbol, its radio button. In v0.6.2: yes for the first two; the double symbol came with the redesign.
- **A question switched off by the personal data answer says so.** Under its code: "Your team answered No to the personal data question, so this question does not apply." The close report does not say whether v0.6.2 has the same fault.
- **The position counts every question.** "Question {n} of {m} in {stage}" counts every question of the stage, switched off or not, the same number All questions, the project page and Home give; no two questions share a place. Previous and Next still skip a switched-off question, so the position can jump by more than one. The close report does not say whether v0.6.2 has the same fault.
- **A way on after a save.** At the foot of the form: "Next question: {the next question in words}" and "All questions", or on a stage's last question "This is the last question in this stage.". On a phone it sits directly under the form. The close report does not say whether v0.6.2 has the same fault.

### What changes underneath

- A kit of shared components (`components/ui`) with its own stylesheets, and colour, type and motion tokens.
- The typeface Overpass in place of Source Sans 3 and DM Serif Display, served from the image (`app/fonts`), so no page fetches a font from elsewhere.
- The vocabulary file `data/vocabulary.json` and its generator (`scripts/vocabulary/generate.mjs`, run at build time, not by the app).
- Copy modules that hold each area's words, with lints that keep words out of the components.
- The document fixes, in `lib/documents/resolve.ts`, `lib/documents/copy.ts` and `lib/documents/render/frame.tsx` (the title's own line height).
- New reads, each inside the organisation's scope: the person's last save, for Home's next step and the project page's main button (`lib/home/`, on an existing index, no schema change); the open findings and mitigations of a question, for a reader; the stage's questions, for the route column; and, for a person in two or more organisations, one more read per signed-in page to keep the side menu's organisation. No API route, permission, schema or stored data changes.
- Test tooling: the protected-test and replacement ledgers, the defect-stage scripts, and a CI change that fetches the full history in the unit job (`.github/workflows/ci.yml`).
- A development gallery of the kit, `/dev/ui-kit`, which answers 404 unless the environment is development or test or the test seam is open (`lib/ui/devGallery.ts`; read in the code, not checked on a production build for these notes).

There is no migration. `git diff v0.6.2..e4f0452d --stat -- prisma docker package.json package-lock.json .env.example` lists two files, `package.json` and `package-lock.json`, each changing only the version line (one line and two lines). So nothing under `prisma/` or `docker/` changes, no dependency changes, and `.env.example` does not change. The production compose file, the nginx configuration, the content version (5.1.0) and the legal texts (version 2) are unchanged. No key is added, removed or renamed in this release: the only `process.env` names the diff adds are `NODE_ENV`, which already existed, and `VOCABULARY_SOURCE`, which only the build-time vocabulary generator reads. One file the host reads from its checkout changes: `ops/host/break-glass-notice.txt`, the text of the emergency-access email. 8.3 step 1 brings it, and nothing is installed.
