## What is released

v0.7.0 is the redesign of the platform's screens, built on v0.6.2 (tag `v0.6.2`), which is the release the host runs now. The version in `package.json` is `0.7.0`.

Every screen keeps its present layout: no screen is restructured. The screens below are reworded and restyled in the structure they have today. Nothing about the records changes: no stored decision, finding, seal or issued document is changed by this release, and no legal text changes, so nobody is asked to accept the texts again.

### What a person will see

- **A new frame and a new look.** A new typeface (Overpass) and a shared set of buttons, fields, messages and page headers on the screens below. A side menu on each signed-in page leads to Home, Projects, Findings, Guidance and Help; Findings opens on the project the person last opened. Every page has its own title in the browser tab.
- **Help.** A new Help page explains the words the platform uses and what sealing means, including that the platform keeps a seal and that no outside body time-stamps it. It opens for everyone, signed in or not, and for a person who has not yet accepted the legal texts.
- **Signing in and joining.** The sign-in, password, invitation and acceptance screens are reworded and restyled, and so is the way the legal texts are shown (the texts themselves are unchanged).
- **My account**, the organisation chooser, the error pages, the fixed messages of the platform's requests and the platform's emails are reworded. So is the email sent when emergency access to the server is used.
- **Recording decisions.** Home, the projects list, the project form, the project page, the list of all questions, a question's page and its history are reworded and restyled.
- **Findings and mitigations.** The findings list and a finding's page are reworded and restyled. A mitigation closed because its decision changed or was withdrawn now says so on the finding's page.
- **Review and seal.** The screens to open a stage review, the review and the seal page are reworded and restyled.
- **Documents.** The documents screen is reworded and restyled ("Create a document"), and the words printed in a new data card, model card and mitigation plan are reworded, including the statement of what a document is and is not. Documents already issued keep their bytes. A document no longer fails to build when one of its mitigations was closed by a changed or withdrawn decision (register D-408): it prints "Closed because the decision changed." or "Closed because the decision was withdrawn.".
- **Download records.** The pages that download an organisation's or a project's records are reworded and restyled. The downloaded file is unchanged.
- **Members, Organisation details and the administrator's pages** are reworded and restyled.
- **Sections the copy review ruled out are removed.** About loses "Why there is no score", "What this platform never does" and the UNCST table, and its "Free toolkits" text becomes the introduction of the controls page (`/explore/controls`); the UNCST table also leaves the documents screen. Home and the project page lose their East Africa Time note, and the "Show UTC" control and the content version line leave the screen that showed them. Times on some other screens are still labelled "EAT".

### What is deliberately not in this release

- A restructure of any screen, including the new Home and the new recording workspace.
- The rewording of the Guidance and About pages. They keep their present words, apart from the sections removed above.
- Definitions shown beside a term the first time a screen uses it. The browser check of first use still marks them as work to do (`test.fail()` in `e2e/vocabulary-first-use.spec.ts`). The retired words the vocabulary lint looks for are gone from the copy, the field maps and the emails, so that lint (`__tests__/lint/vocabulary.test.ts`) now passes as an ordinary case.
- The screen-reader walk of the main journeys: see Owner waivers.

### What changes underneath

- A kit of shared components (`components/ui`) with its own stylesheets, and colour, type and motion tokens.
- The typeface Overpass in place of Source Sans 3 and DM Serif Display, served from the image (`app/fonts`), so no page fetches a font from elsewhere.
- The vocabulary file `data/vocabulary.json` and its generator (`scripts/vocabulary/generate.mjs`, run at build time, not by the app).
- Copy modules that hold each area's words, with lints that keep words out of the components.
- The D-408 fix, in `lib/documents/resolve.ts` only.
- Test tooling: the protected-test and replacement ledgers, the defect-stage scripts, and a CI change that fetches the full history in the unit job (`.github/workflows/ci.yml`).
- A development gallery of the kit, `/dev/ui-kit`, which answers 404 unless the environment is development or test or the test seam is open (`lib/ui/devGallery.ts`; read in the code, not checked on a production build for these notes).

There is no migration. `git diff v0.6.2..b082c22d --stat -- prisma docker package.json package-lock.json .env.example` lists two files, `package.json` and `package-lock.json`, each changing only the version line (one line and two lines). So nothing under `prisma/` or `docker/` changes, no dependency changes, and `.env.example` does not change. The production compose file, the nginx configuration, the content version (5.1.0) and the legal texts (version 2) are unchanged. No key is added, removed or renamed in this release: the only `process.env` names the diff adds are `NODE_ENV`, which already existed, and `VOCABULARY_SOURCE`, which only the build-time vocabulary generator reads. One file the host reads from its checkout changes: `ops/host/break-glass-notice.txt`, the text of the emergency-access email. 8.3 step 1 brings it, and nothing is installed.
