## The incident in the rehearsal

Two GET requests of the rehearsal reached the production address on 12 November 2026 at 18:44:13 to 18:44:14 UTC, through a test-tool call that runs outside the browser's pin to the local stack. Both were answered 401 `unauthenticated`. They most likely carried a session cookie valid only on the local stack. No production data was read or written: this is inferred from the 401 answers. What may have left the machine: a local project id, the organisation short name `makrai-synthetic` and a session token valid only on the local stack. The call was removed. It is the second such incident (the first was in the v0.6.0 live run, on 6 November 2026), so ADR-0021 was broken again by the same class of call. This is register row D-335, which asks for a mechanism in place of a rule. Production's logs were not checked for them: asked on 12 November 2026, the owner answered "No need".
